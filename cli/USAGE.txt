MH ARKit Export - standalone DNA baker

Extract the entire download, then open a terminal in its directory.
Python, Unreal Engine, Blender and compiler installations are not required.

Linux / macOS:
  ./mh-arkit-export head.dna --body-dna body.dna -o character.glb

Windows PowerShell:
  .\mh-arkit-export.exe head.dna --body-dna body.dna -o character.glb

Quote paths containing spaces. Omit --body-dna for a head-only export.
The body is included in its neutral pose, without a skeleton or body animation.
The output includes geometry, UVs, and 52 facial morph targets. Textures and
materials are not included. Use head and body DNA from the same character.

Diagnostics:
  mh-arkit-export --version
  mh-arkit-export --build-info
  mh-arkit-export --self-test

The self-test generates a tiny synthetic rig and verifies DNA loading,
RigLogic, joint skinning, corrective shapes, and GLB output. It needs no
download or character files and does not alter your DNA.

Use --help to see mesh selection and custom expression-mapping options.
Exact parity with Unreal's AS_MetaHuman_ARKit_Mapping is not yet verified.
Combining individually baked shapes approximates nonlinear rig interactions.

Keep the executable and its _internal directory together. MANIFEST.json
records the build inputs, dependency versions, compatibility, and file hashes.
Verify the archive checksum before distributing it. For signed/notarized
releases, follow the publisher's platform-specific installation instructions.
